
Title: Employee Password Reset Guide
Department: IT Service Desk

Problem:
Employees cannot log in because their password has expired
or they have forgotten their password.

Troubleshooting Steps:
1. Open the company password reset portal.
2. Select Forgot Password.
3. Enter the company email address.
4. Complete the identity verification process.
5. Create a new password.
6. Sign in again using the new password.

Important:
Never share your password with another employee.

Incident Reference:
INC-1002
