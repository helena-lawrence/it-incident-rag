
Title: VPN Troubleshooting Guide
Department: IT Network Support

Problem:
Employees cannot connect to the company VPN.

Common Causes:
1. Incorrect username or password.
2. Internet connection is unavailable.
3. VPN client is outdated.
4. Company VPN server is temporarily unavailable.

Troubleshooting Steps:
1. Check that the internet connection is working.
2. Restart the VPN application.
3. Verify the username and password.
4. Check whether the VPN client requires an update.
5. Restart the computer.
6. If the issue continues, contact IT Network Support.

Incident Reference:
INC-1001

Error 809:
If VPN Error 809 appears, contact the Network Support Team.
